Yehyun Suh

My name is Yehyun, and I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Vanderbilt University and an affiliated student at VISE(Vanderbilt Institute for Surgery and Engineering) and VALIANT(Vanderbilt Lab for Immersive AI Translation).

I am currently doing my research in VINE(Vision Information Neuroimaging Engineering) Laboratory under the supervision of Dr. Daniel Moyer. I am also collaborating closely with Dr. J. Ryan Martin in Department of Orthopaedic Surgery at Vanderbilt University Medical Center. My main focus in my research is in medical image registration and application of Deep Learning in medical images. Before my graduate studies, I finished my undergraduate in Information and Communication Engineering at Dongguk University under the advise of Dr. Woongsup Kim.
