Yehyun is an [NIH F31 Predoctoral Fellow](https://lnkd.in/p/eXfQacTk) and a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Vanderbilt University](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/)  and an affiliated student at ⁠[VISE](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/vise/)(Vanderbilt Institute for Surgery and Engineering) and [VALIANT](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/valiant/)(Vanderbilt Lab for Immersive AI Translation).

He conducts his research in the [VINE](https://vine-lab.notion.site/) (Vision Information Neuroimaging Engineering) Laboratory under the supervision of [Dr. Daniel Moyer](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=sKmoxSMAAAAJ&hl). He also collaborates closely with [Dr. J. Ryan Martin](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/J-Martin-10) in the Department of Orthopaedic Surgery at Vanderbilt University Medical Center, [Dr. Yayun Du](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LPwbf4kAAAAJ&hl=en) in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Vanderbilt University, and [Dr. Alexander P. Hughes](https://www.hss.edu/profiles/doctors/alexander-hughes) from Hospital of Special Surgery. His research focuses on machine learning and computational methods across medical image registration and analysis, computer vision, and IMU-based time-series data. In addition to his research, Yehyun serves as Vice President of the Korean Graduate Students and Scholars Association (KSSA) and as Treasurer of VALIANT.

Yehyun previously worked as a Research Intern at AlphaTEC Spine, where he worked with [Dr. Kongbin Kang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kongbin-kang-177b0ab), [Dr. Lin Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fOwSWgkAAAAJ&hl=en), and [Dr. Chaochao Zhou](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=PYcUZ3YAAAAJ&hl=en). He also conducted research as a Research Intern at Seoul National University Hospital (SNUH), working with [Dr. Jisun Hwang](https://www.researchgate.net/scientific-contributions/Jisun-Hwang-2161036940) and [Dr. Yisak Kim](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Zs9ba6oAAAAJ&hl=en). He received his undergraduate degree in Information and Communication Engineering from Dongguk University, where he conducted research under the supervision of [Dr. Woongsup Kim](https://ice.dongguk.edu/professor/list?professor_haggwa_type=PROFH_042).
